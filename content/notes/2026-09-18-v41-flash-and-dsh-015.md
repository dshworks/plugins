Two things moved under dsh in the same week: the model most sessions run on,
and the harness version `npx @deepseek-ai/dsh` gives you.

## The model

On 2026-09-10 DeepSeek released V4.1 Flash and retired V4 Flash and V4 Flash
Vision Exp ([change log](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/updates/)). The model
name is now `deepseek-flash`. The old names `deepseek-v4-flash` and
`deepseek-v4-flash-vision-exp` are still accepted: their requests are served
by V4.1 Flash and billed at the Flash price
([pricing, note 1](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/quick_start/pricing/)).
V4 Pro continues after 2026-09-14 with billing unchanged (note 2).

dsh already moved. 0.1.5-rc.2 ships `deepseek-flash` as the default agent
model, where 0.1.2-rc.1 shipped `deepseek-v4-flash`
([0.1.5-rc.2](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness/blob/dsh-v0.1.5-rc.2/packages/bundle/base/cordis.patch.yml#L79),
[0.1.2-rc.1](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness/blob/dsh-v0.1.2-rc.1/packages/bundle/base/cordis.patch.yml#L79)).
A `settings.yaml` that still names `deepseek-v4-flash` keeps working and is
billed as Flash. DeepSeek calls the routing temporary, so change the name the
next time you touch the file.

## The price

Flash only, per 1M tokens, off-peak. Peak is 2x and the windows did not move:
Monday to Friday, 01:00-04:00 and 06:00-10:00 UTC.

| Flash, off-peak | cache hit | cache miss | output |
|---|---|---|---|
| USD before | 0.007 | 0.22 | 0.66 |
| USD now | 0.003 | 0.15 | 0.6 |
| CNY before | 0.05 | 1.5 | 4.5 |
| CNY now | 0.02 | 1 | 4 |

"Now" is the [en](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/quick_start/pricing/) and
[zh-cn](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/zh-cn/quick_start/pricing/) pricing pages
as read on 2026-09-18. "Before" is the same pages as our docs mirror captured
them on 2026-08-24
([en](https://github.com/thevibeworks/deepseek-docs/blob/a5c4c55c00a840015ca579bfd72cc653658c509e/content/en/quick_start/pricing.md),
[zh-cn](https://github.com/thevibeworks/deepseek-docs/blob/a5c4c55c00a840015ca579bfd72cc653658c509e/content/zh-cn/quick_start/pricing.md)).
V4 Pro is unchanged at 0.022 / 0.66 / 1.98 USD off-peak.

What a cost figure should read now: all three Flash names priced on the new
row. A meter still carrying the old card overstates a Flash session by 2.3x
on cached input, 1.5x on uncached input and 1.1x on output. The cut is not the
same in both currencies: cached input fell 57% in USD and 60% in CNY, output 9%
and 11%.

Our own meter now does this: dsh-meter 0.5.0 prices `deepseek-flash` on the
new row and bills the two legacy names as Flash
([release notes](https://github.com/dshworks/dsh-meter/releases/tag/v0.5.0)).
Versions before 0.5.0 carry the old card.

## dsh 0.1.5 on latest

> **Corrected 2026-09-29.** This section first said that 132 of 1,276
> published packages accepted dsh 0.1.5-rc.2, that our four plugins were "in
> the broken column" until re-released, that an older install ran against a
> second copy of the harness, and that a range cannot be widened ahead of a
> release. All four came from modelling an install as a fresh npm tree. dsh
> installs plugins with pnpm, never installs a peer, and hands a plugin the
> host's own harness; and dsh 0.1.5-rc.2 did not read a plugin's range at
> all. [The correction](https://dsh.works/notes/installs-correction) has the
> sources and the re-measured counts.

This site's nightly census first read npm's `latest` tag as 0.1.5-rc.1 on
2026-09-10, and as 0.1.5-rc.2 on 2026-09-18. `alpha` is already at
0.1.6-alpha.2.

Our four did ship releases that morning adding `|| ^0.1.5-rc.1` to their dsh
peer ranges: dsh-meter 0.5.0, dsh-watch 0.2.2, dsh-crew 0.2.2 and
dsh-ego-browser 0.1.2. Nothing in dsh 0.1.5 read the range, so the versions
before them were not refused either, and there was nothing to update for.
